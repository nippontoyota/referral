# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Nippon Toyota staff use the admin area to manage customer referrals and WhatsApp outreach. Existing customers arrive from WhatsApp on mobile and submit referrals for friends interested in a Glanza or Hyryder.

## Product Purpose

Give staff one reliable workflow to import customers, send personalized referral links, track delivery progress, review referrals, and export results. Give customers a fast, clear referral form that works well on phones.

## Brand Personality

Confident, clear, restrained. The interface should feel recognizably Nippon Toyota without distracting staff or customers from their task.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, decorative gradients and shadows, dense desktop-only tables, multiple competing primary actions, campaign-management complexity, and unfamiliar form controls.

## Design Principles

- Keep each screen focused on one primary task.
- Make critical status and recovery actions immediately understandable.
- Preserve familiar admin and form conventions.
- Optimize the public journey for WhatsApp users on small screens.
- Use brand color to guide action, not as decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast and keyboard requirements, keep touch targets at least 44px, preserve visible focus states, support reduced motion, and prevent horizontal scrolling at 320px.
